01 · Opportunity

From passive catalog to managed commerce

Balensed already has a Shopify store, a substantial product catalog, available inventory, and prior selling activity. The opportunity is to connect these assets through a disciplined commercial system.

Preliminary finding

Observed

Current store activity is not translating into consistent sales. Early review shows a meaningful gap between reported traffic and actual customer purchases, making traffic quality, inventory pathways, merchandising, trust, and measurement immediate priorities.

Program objective

90-day focus

Build a focused ecommerce operation that helps qualified customers discover the right available products, understand their value, move confidently through checkout, and return through structured follow-up.

Primary business goals

  • Prioritize inventory with the strongest sales potential
  • Improve the customer journey from discovery through checkout
  • Reduce traffic loss to unavailable or low-priority products
  • Strengthen product presentation, trust, and mobile usability
  • Increase qualified visibility across Shopify, Google, and relevant digital channels
  • Establish measurable lifecycle marketing and AI-assisted growth operations

02 · Phase 1 · Week 1

Diagnostic, strategy & inventory priorities

We begin with evidence. Before major changes, E4LA will establish the commercial baseline, identify constraints, and determine where implementation can create the most value.

Business & performance review

  • Shopify sales, traffic, conversion, and customer behavior
  • Traffic-source quality and conversion drop-off
  • Historical product performance where data is available
  • Existing channels, discounts, automations, apps, and recurring costs
  • Shipping, returns, payments, markets, and public customer policies

Catalog & inventory assessment

  • Products, variants, collections, product types, vendors, tags, and availability
  • Available, sold-out, low-stock, inactive, and fragmented-size products
  • Highly viewed products that are unavailable or not converting
  • Physical inventory reconciliation based on information supplied by Balensed
  • Priority framework for sell, improve, test, archive, or hold decisions

Phase 1 deliverables

Foundation

A diagnostic summary, baseline metrics, inventory opportunity assessment, priority-product list, conversion-risk findings, and an implementation roadmap for the remainder of the 90-day engagement.

03 · Phase 2 · Weeks 2–5

Shopify conversion & customer experience improvement

E4LA will improve the existing storefront without unnecessarily rebuilding Shopify from the beginning or disrupting secure native checkout functionality.

Storefront experience

  • Refine homepage structure and primary messaging
  • Improve navigation, collections, filters, and product discovery
  • Strengthen mobile shopping and calls to action
  • Clarify availability, shipping, returns, and customer support
  • Reduce unnecessary visibility of unavailable products
  • Review cart and public checkout usability

Priority product optimization

Up to 20 priority products will receive focused optimization based on commercial potential.

  • Titles, descriptions, product types, collections, and tags
  • Size, fit, material, care, and availability information
  • Search metadata and essential product attributes
  • Image order, internal recommendations, and purchase pathways

Trust & consistency

Customer-facing

Conflicting, unclear, duplicated, or unsupported customer-facing messages will be identified and corrected with Balensed approval. Working checkout and operational systems will be preserved and verified.

04 · Phase 3 · Weeks 4–10

Visibility, merchandising & sales activation

Once the foundation is stronger, E4LA will prepare priority inventory for qualified discovery and controlled commercial testing.

Product visibility

  • Review Shopify product-feed and Google Merchant Center readiness
  • Identify missing attributes, feed errors, or product disapprovals
  • Improve product SEO, structured information, and collection visibility
  • Strengthen eligibility for Google free product listings where applicable
  • Improve internal linking and discoverability of priority products

Sales activation

  • Limited-inventory and merchandising strategy
  • Bundle, archive, final-size, or no-restock opportunities
  • Email capture and eligible customer reactivation
  • Browse, cart, and checkout abandonment workflows where supported
  • Campaign framework for priority products
  • Controlled testing before significant advertising investment

Acquisition principle

The program will prioritize qualified customer activity, product engagement, and completed purchases—not inflated traffic or vanity metrics. Paid advertising, if recommended, will require a separate approved media budget.

05 · AI-assisted operations

Smarter monitoring, faster decisions

E4LA will configure a practical operating system using appropriate Shopify capabilities and AI-assisted workflows—not an unsupervised system that changes the business on its own.

AI-supported capabilities

  • Catalog quality and inventory opportunity monitoring
  • Detection of highly viewed products producing no sales
  • Identification of unavailable products receiving traffic
  • Product-content and merchandising assistance
  • Campaign planning and weekly performance analysis
  • Recommendations for offers, bundles, prioritization, or testing

Human approval remains central

AI will not independently change prices, publish discounts, activate preorders, purchase advertising, alter inventory, publish unapproved content, contact customers, or make financial and contractual decisions.

  • AI supports research and execution
  • E4LA verifies outputs and recommendations
  • Balensed approves material business decisions

06 · Weeks 6–12

Measurement, optimization & next-stage plan

Performance will be reviewed against the initial baseline so decisions are based on verified customer behavior and inventory movement.

DiscoverQualified sessions, product views, landing pages, and traffic-source quality.
ConsiderAdd-to-cart activity, priority-product engagement, and abandonment behavior.
ConvertCheckout activity, completed orders, revenue, average order value, and sell-through.
ImproveWeekly priorities, controlled tests, corrective action, and next-stage recommendations.

End-of-program deliverable

Balensed will receive a summary of completed improvements, performance against the initial baseline, priority-product and inventory findings, remaining dependencies, and a practical roadmap for continued growth.

08 · Scope & responsibilities

What is required—and what is separate

Clear responsibilities protect the schedule, working systems, and commercial integrity of the engagement.

Balensed will provide

  • Appropriate Shopify and relevant platform access
  • Accurate physical inventory and product availability information
  • Product cost or margin information when required for pricing decisions
  • Product specifications, care information, and original brand assets
  • Timely approval of customer-facing and commercial decisions
  • Reliable fulfillment and customer service for incoming orders

Not included

  • Advertising spend, creator fees, or marketplace charges
  • Professional photography, video, models, locations, or styling
  • Paid Shopify themes, applications, manufacturing, or fulfillment
  • Full manual rewriting of every product and variant
  • Custom software or fully custom AI application development
  • Legal, trademark, tax, accounting, or regulatory services

Approvals and additional work

Change control

Pricing, inventory, payment, shipping, advertising expenditure, public positioning, and customer communication changes require Balensed approval. Work outside the approved scope may require a revised timeline, separate estimate, or written authorization.

09 · Proposed outcome

A stronger foundation for measurable growth

The program is designed to improve the conditions for sales while establishing a more disciplined way to manage the store after the initial engagement.

By the end of the initial 90 days, Balensed should have

A clearer inventory and ecommerce strategy; a more organized Shopify storefront; a stronger mobile customer experience; improved priority-product presentation; more reliable measurement; stronger product-visibility foundations; better customer follow-up workflows; an AI-assisted operating system; and a prioritized plan for continued growth.

Sales performance depends on product demand, available sizes, pricing, product quality, content, customer trust, shipping, fulfillment, customer service, seasonality, platform conditions, and any approved marketing investment. E4LA does not guarantee a specific sales volume, revenue amount, advertising result, or search ranking.
