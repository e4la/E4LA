03 · Digital ecosystem

Multiple brands create opportunity—and require governance.

The public ecosystem serves overlapping audiences across surgical, nonsurgical, destination, recovery and skincare journeys. Its roles should be explicit for patients, staff, search engines and reporting.

What E4LA will clarify

  • Which domain supports each service category
  • Which brand owns each patient journey
  • Which phone and booking path serve each department
  • How surgical and medspa enquiries are routed
  • How local and destination patients are handled
  • How analytics connect across the ecosystem
  • How search and AI systems understand the relationships

Why it matters

  • Reduce patient confusion
  • Protect specialist surgical positioning
  • Prevent overlapping content from competing unnecessarily
  • Improve attribution across forms, calls and bookings
  • Create clearer ownership for follow-up
  • Support accurate local and entity signals
  • Make future investment easier to evaluate

Website decision

The current website contains substantial authority and content. E4LA will improve the priority templates and patient journeys first. A full rebuild will only be recommended if the existing platform materially prevents the required tracking, conversion, performance or privacy improvements.

04 · Patient journeys

Different decisions require different conversion experiences.

The initial engagement will prioritize one surgical journey and one secondary medspa or destination journey, selected after reviewing demand, capacity and business priorities.

Surgical journey
Search, social or referral → procedure or concern page → clinical differentiation → matched patient evidence → recovery and travel confidence → private consultation → human follow-up
Medspa journey
Local discovery or social content → concern or treatment page → provider and expectations → booking or consultation → treatment → rebooking → appropriate maintenance plan
Shared measurement
Source → qualified enquiry → consultation or booking → attendance → treatment or procedure → rebooking → review or referral

07 · AI-assisted operations

Use AI for intelligence—not clinical judgment.

The operating layer supports research, monitoring, content operations, lead visibility and reporting. It does not act as an autonomous clinical voice.

Monitor

Search and entity signals, content opportunities, performance changes, reputation themes and funnel leakage where approved data is available.

Assist

Research briefs, transcript repurposing, draft content, lead-source classification and executive performance summaries.

Protect

No diagnosis, candidacy assessment, postoperative advice, unapproved patient data, unreviewed clinical publication or autonomous material budget change.

08 · Measurement

Evaluate patient outcomes—not activity alone.

Reporting begins with visibility and engagement, but the management objective is to connect marketing activity to consultations, attendance, treatment, retention and reputation.

VisibilityPriority search visibility, local discovery, branded demand, qualified social reach and AI-search observations.
EngagementPriority-page interaction, proof engagement, video quality, consultation actions, booking starts and social-to-site traffic.
AcquisitionQualified calls, forms, direct messages, consultation requests, medspa bookings and source quality.
ConversionLead-to-consultation, booking-to-attendance, consultation-to-treatment, abandonment, no-show and cancellation.
Patient valueCompleted treatments, completed procedures, approved revenue signals, rebooking, repeat activity and referrals.
ReputationReview recency, response coverage, recurring themes, patient-experience concerns and referral signals.

09 · Scope & accountability

Clear delivery limits protect execution quality.

The monthly fee supports a focused operating system. Work beyond the defined limits is reviewed and approved before implementation.

Included in stage one

  • Strategic planning and monthly leadership
  • One monthly executive review
  • Approximately four priority page or template interventions
  • Two primary patient-journey improvements
  • Analytics and attribution within supported systems
  • One focused SEO, content or technical cycle per month
  • Up to three approved routing or follow-up workflows
  • One controlled paid-search campaign group after readiness
  • Monthly reporting

Separate approval required

  • Complete website rebuild
  • Unlimited development or custom software
  • CRM replacement or enterprise migration
  • Multiple large advertising platforms
  • Onsite photography or video production
  • High-volume original medical content
  • Daily Stories or live community management
  • Influencer partnerships
  • Legal or medical compliance services

Practice responsibilities
