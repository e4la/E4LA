What We Need to Fix

The main growth risks are clear.

The proposal has been red-teamed around the ways this engagement could fail commercially, operationally or technically.

Owner Journey Is Not Clear Enough

The public website still gives significant attention to tenant needs. Owner acquisition needs a clearer path from discovery to consultation.

Visibility Can Exist Without Revenue

Better rankings or more traffic do not automatically produce new managed properties. Attribution and conversion must be built into the system.

Follow-Up Can Become the Bottleneck

A qualified owner can still be lost through slow response, missed follow-up or unclear next steps.

Technical Issues Can Reduce Trust

Navigation, mobile usability, forms, property data, indexing and owner-facing interactions must work reliably before traffic is increased.

Too Many Audiences Can Dilute the Message

Initial acquisition should focus on owners ready to hire, owners ready to switch, and remote/investor owners.

AI Can Become Scope Creep

The Growth Agent must stay focused on revenue-relevant monitoring rather than becoming a large technology project.

Owner Lead Generation

Lead generation comes from multiple channels—not one source.

BPM's growth system is designed to find, attract, capture and qualify property owners before they enter the sales pipeline. The objective is not more inquiries; it is more qualified owners with a real management need.

Channel 1

High-Intent Google Search

Capture owners already searching for property management, rental management, switching managers, landlord help or out-of-area management in Bakersfield.

Search traffic is directed to owner-focused landing pages, Rental Analysis or Consultation—not generic website traffic.
Channel 2

Rental Analysis Funnel

Create a lead-capture path where an owner provides the property address, property type, current rent, occupancy, current management situation, main concern, timeline and contact information.

Why it matters: the funnel generates the lead and helps BPM qualify the opportunity before the first conversation.
Channel 3

Switch-to-BPM Campaigns

Target owners dissatisfied with their current manager because of poor communication, slow maintenance, weak reporting, tenant problems or lack of responsiveness.

Channel 4

Investor & Landlord Communities

Build Omid's presence in relevant Bakersfield and Kern County investor/landlord communities through education, useful market insight, networking, Q&A and relationship building—not mass promotion.

Channel 5

Out-of-Area & Absentee Owners

Identify and reach owners who hold Bakersfield rental property but live elsewhere, using appropriate public or licensed data sources and targeted outreach rather than mass spam.

Channel 6

Professional Referral Partners

Develop referral relationships with Realtors, investment agents, mortgage professionals, lenders, CPAs, insurance professionals, contractors, 1031-exchange professionals and other trusted owner-facing partners.

Channel 7

Instagram + Facebook Acquisition

Use owner education, investor content, Omid authority, reviews and short-form video to move qualified audiences toward Rental Analysis, Consultation, website conversion or direct inquiry.

Channel 8

Existing Lead Reactivation

Where usable records exist, re-engage previous inquiries, old prospects, brokerage relationships, former opportunities and owners who previously showed interest but did not sign.

AI Growth & Revenue Agent

Version 1 stays focused on business impact.

The agent is not a chatbot and does not replace the team. It monitors the growth system and surfaces issues that require action.

1. Website Health

Flag broken forms, owner-path problems, outdated information and critical public-site issues.

2. Search Opportunity

Identify meaningful owner-query changes, lost visibility and new commercial search opportunities.

3. Lead Follow-Up

Surface qualified leads that do not receive timely action or require another follow-up.

4. Competitor Intelligence

Monitor meaningful changes from Ascend and selected competitors without turning every competitor update into noise.

5. Growth Summary

Answer: What changed? What is working? What is not? Where is the next opportunity? What should BPM do next?

Security by Design

Agent permissions begin narrowly. Sensitive tenant/legal information is not unnecessarily ingested, and high-impact actions are not automated without explicit approval.

Least privilege means a system receives only the minimum access it needs to perform its job.

Risk Controls

Designed to avoid the most likely failure modes.

Before launch, the strategy was reviewed from a failure-first perspective so the system does not become busy work without commercial evidence.

Critical Risk
Marketing activity increases but managed properties do not.
Response: keep New Managed Properties as the primary KPI and trace every major activity to the owner funnel.
Critical Risk
New clients cannot be attributed to a source.
Response: source tracking and CRM pipeline are established before campaigns scale.
High Risk
Qualified owners enter the system but do not close.
Response: measure Lead → Consultation → Agreement and improve the stage where conversion breaks.
High Risk
Month 1 becomes an oversized redesign/technology project.
Response: only implement changes that directly support owner acquisition, measurement, trust or conversion.
