01 · Foundation

Business assessment & brand direction

The first month focused on diagnosis, correcting inefficient activity, offer development, content preparation, and building the foundation required for later optimization.

Business & digital assessment

Completed

E4LA reviewed WeHo Grill as a connected customer-acquisition ecosystem rather than a social-media-only account.

  • Website and menu alignment
  • Google Business Profile and local discovery
  • Google Ads and Meta/Instagram advertising
  • OpenTable, ezCater, DoorDash and Uber Eats
  • Reviews, catering, group ordering and digital customer journey

Strategic positioning

Established

The working direction became Modern Persian Hospitality in Los Angeles, extending the restaurant beyond a generic Persian/Mediterranean category label.

  • Persian hospitality and cultural authenticity
  • Grilled specialties and regional Persian cuisine
  • Quality, freshness and local relevance
  • Catering and group-ordering visibility

Google Business Profile, local search & advertising

Work began on both organic local visibility and paid acquisition, but the full 90-day testing and optimization cycle was not available before cancellation.

Google Business Profile & local search

In progress

  • Halal food / Halal Persian food
  • Koobideh / kabob / Persian kabob
  • Persian catering / corporate catering / group meals
  • Specific Persian dishes, menu products and nearby restaurant intent

Advertising reset

Launched, not fully optimized

E4LA stopped the previous Google advertising approach and began rebuilding paid acquisition around more commercially meaningful customer intent, including catering, group ordering, menu offers and new-customer demand. The revised Google budget was approximately $600. The relationship ended before the replacement campaign received the planned testing and optimization period.

03 · Social & paid media

Instagram strategy & early creative testing

The project ended before a full sequential Instagram program could be deployed. Only a limited number of planned pieces were published and promoted.

Meta / Instagram advertising review

Previous spend stopped

The prior ongoing Instagram advertising level was approximately $2,000 per month. E4LA canceled that approach and shifted toward smaller controlled creative tests tied to specific content and commercial objectives.

Three initial promoted creatives

Early test only

Approximately $80 total was allocated to three initial promoted pieces.

  • Appetizer series: photo-led introduction to WeHo Grill appetizers
  • Sambuseh: short-form video using a fun concept
  • Kabob Torsh: culturally driven video centered on Gilan / Northern Iran and regional authenticity

Publishing & responsibility model

Defined

E4LA established a working division between strategic/revenue-focused campaign activity and the restaurant’s day-to-day organic social activity to reduce conflicting promotion decisions and preserve clearer campaign attribution.

04 · Commercial offer development

Catering strategy, menu architecture & visual system

Catering became one of the strongest commercial opportunities identified during the first phase and received significant strategy, menu, packaging and visual-development work.

Catering architecture

Developed

The customer-facing structure was reorganized around a clearer group-ordering hierarchy.

  • Catering packages
  • Boxed lunches
  • Sandwich boxes
  • Group trays
  • Rice, salads and Persian sides
  • Mezze, desserts and service items

Menu development

Multiple rounds completed

E4LA worked through package architecture, product combinations, portion structures, quantities, naming, descriptions, boxed-meal formats, sandwich formats, trays, operational considerations and platform presentation.

Pricing dependency

Blocked at closeout

Final revised restaurant pricing was requested but was not received before the engagement ended. Some catering materials therefore remained based on previously supplied pricing and final synchronization could not be completed.

Sandwich-box product development

Developed

Dedicated group-order concepts were prepared for Koobideh, Juicy Chicken and Falafel sandwiches, with organized sides such as Shirazi Salad, pickles and sauce for convenient 10-, 20-, 30-person and larger orders.

Catering & food visual production

Substantial production completed

Multiple visual iterations were produced for kabobs, Juicy Chicken, mixed grill, premium kabob, falafel, rice, salads, hummus, Persian appetizers, desserts, boxed meals and sandwich boxes, emphasizing recognizable, brand-faithful food presentation and practical catering packaging.

05 · Production

Prepared content inventory at cancellation

Content had been prepared in advance for the next phase, with paid distribution intended to follow a staged rather than random posting sequence.

Edited video inventory

Prepared, unpublished

Approximately 5–6 edited video assets remained prepared for later publication and advertising when the engagement ended.

Photo / content inventory

Prepared, unpublished

More than 10 photo/content assets had also been prepared in anticipation of continuing the roadmap into the following month.

Roadmap dependency

Sequence interrupted

The unpublished inventory was created as part of a connected content-and-advertising sequence. Cancellation occurred before those assets could be deployed, tested and evaluated within the planned campaign cycle.

06 · Platform alignment

ezCater, Toast preparation & customer touchpoints

Platform work was underway but should not be represented as fully completed because final pricing and later roadmap implementation were still outstanding.

ezCater restructuring

Partially implemented

E4LA reviewed and reorganized the catering structure around packages, boxes, trays and sides, including descriptions, customer-ordering flow, minimum-order considerations and delivery logic. Final updating remained incomplete because current pricing was not supplied before closeout.

Toast Catering & Events

Training completed

E4LA completed the training/research required to incorporate Toast Catering & Events into the following month of the roadmap. The engagement was canceled before implementation began.

Cross-platform alignment

In progress

The roadmap included consistency work across Google Business Profile, Google Ads, website, Instagram, ezCater, Toast, DoorDash, Uber Eats, catering materials and online ordering. Work began on naming, pricing, descriptions, photography, offers and customer pathways, but full synchronization was not reached.

07 · Authority & relationships

Reviews, reputation & creator direction

These areas were initiated during the first phase, while deeper outreach and the longer-term reputation cycle had not yet been completed.

Review & reputation strategy

Initiated

E4LA worked on Google review strategy, authentic customer-feedback direction, local trust signals, reputation consistency and the relationship between reviews and local-search visibility.

Creator / influencer direction

Introductions provided

Near the end of the engagement, E4LA introduced several potential Los Angeles food creators/influencers. Had the program continued, E4LA was expected to act as a third-party communication and negotiation representative to simplify collaboration for the restaurant, without taking a percentage of influencer compensation or deals.

08 · Closeout

What the 90-day roadmap did not reach

The engagement ended approximately one-third of the way through the intended program, before sufficient time existed for repeated measurement, iteration and scaling.

Important reporting context

This engagement should not be evaluated as a completed 90-day growth program. Cancellation occurred before later phases involving sustained performance measurement, multi-cycle campaign optimization, full content rollout, accumulated local-search improvement, extended Google Business Profile testing, catering performance analysis, full cross-platform synchronization, creator-collaboration execution and longer-term reputation development.
