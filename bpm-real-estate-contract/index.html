1. Three Business Goals

The partnership is organized around three clear outcomes rather than a long list of disconnected marketing tasks.

Goal 1Switch to BPM

Attract property owners who are dissatisfied with their current property manager and convert qualified prospects into signed BPM management agreements.

Goal 2New Rental Owners

Reach owners who need professional property management and move them from search or referral to consultation and signed management agreement.

Goal 3Investment Opportunities

Present BPM opportunities to relevant Realtors, investors and buyers through dedicated opportunity pages, search visibility, AI-search visibility and targeted Instagram distribution.

Primary business outcomes: signed management agreements, owners who switch to BPM, qualified investment inquiries and closed investment transactions. Rankings, impressions, traffic and social reach are supporting indicators—not the end goal.

2. Execution Roadmap

The work is divided into three practical stages so BPM can understand what is being built and E4LA can manage the work without unnecessary complexity.

Stage 1Protect, Understand & Plan

Audit the existing website, preserve current SEO value, review UX and conversion paths, and define the three priority user journeys.

Stage 2Redesign & Build

Redesign the website experience and build the three priority growth paths: Switch to BPM, New Rental Owners and Investment Opportunities.

Stage 3Launch & Grow

Expand search visibility, AI-search visibility, Omid authority content, opportunity content, Instagram distribution and lead attribution around the three goals.

Performance will be reviewed against business outcomes first, with channel metrics used to diagnose and improve the system.

15. Cancellation / Early Termination

After the initial 90 days, either party may terminate the ongoing engagement for convenience by providing at least 30 days' written notice. The notice must clearly state the requested termination date and allow the parties a reasonable opportunity to address any stated service concern during the notice period. E4LA will continue performing the agreed services through the effective termination date unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

  • BPM remains responsible for fees earned and scheduled through the effective termination date, plus approved third-party costs or non-cancellable commitments incurred for BPM.
  • Amounts already paid for completed work, reserved capacity, or services already performed are non-refundable except where required by law or expressly agreed in writing.
  • No new recurring service fee will be charged for a service period beginning after the effective termination date.
  • After all undisputed amounts due are paid, E4LA will provide BPM's final paid client-specific deliverables and a reasonable handoff of agreed account/access information. E4LA retains its pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, systems and know-how as stated in Section 9.
  • Either party may terminate earlier for a material breach that remains uncured after written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure. E4LA may pause services for failed payments, security concerns, unlawful requests, or loss of required access.

This agreement and written addenda accepted by both parties define the operative scope. Material changes must be documented in writing.
