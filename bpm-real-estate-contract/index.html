3. Owner Lead Generation System

E4LA will not rely on one lead source. The acquisition system will combine multiple channels so BPM can identify which sources produce qualified owners and new managed properties.

Channel 1High-Intent Google Search

Target owners actively searching for property management, rental management, switching managers, landlord help and out-of-area management in Bakersfield.

Channel 2Rental Analysis Funnel

Capture property address, property type, current rent, occupancy, current management situation, owner concern, timeline and contact information to generate and pre-qualify leads.

Channel 3Switch-to-BPM Campaigns

Target owners dissatisfied with communication, maintenance, reporting, responsiveness or tenant-problem handling from their current manager.

Channel 4Investor & Landlord Communities

Build useful presence through education, Q&A, networking, market insight and relationship building rather than mass promotion.

Channel 5Out-of-Area / Absentee Owners

Identify and reach owners who hold Bakersfield rental property but live elsewhere, using appropriate public or licensed data sources and targeted outreach.

Channel 6Professional Referral Partners

Develop referral relationships with Realtors, investment agents, lenders, mortgage professionals, CPAs, insurance professionals, contractors, 1031-exchange professionals and other owner-facing partners.

Channel 7Instagram + Facebook Acquisition

Use owner/investor education, Omid authority, reviews and short-form video to move qualified audiences toward Rental Analysis, Consultation, website conversion or direct inquiry.

Channel 8Existing Lead Reactivation

Where usable records exist, re-engage prior inquiries, old prospects, former opportunities and relevant brokerage relationships that did not convert.

Lead Qualification

Qualification criteria

Service area, ownership status, property count, property type, current manager, urgency/timeline, primary pain point, readiness for consultation and economic fit.

Lead stages

Hot, Qualified, Nurture and Low Priority, with next-action tracking so Omid and BPM can focus effort on the most valuable opportunities.

7. BPM Responsibilities

The growth system depends on reasonable client cooperation and timely access.

Access

BPM will provide reasonable access to the website/CMS, Google Business Profile, analytics/search accounts, social accounts, CRM or lead records, email platform and other agreed systems needed for the work.

Business information

BPM is responsible for providing accurate service, pricing, team, licensing, operational and property-management information used in public content.

Approvals

BPM will provide reasonably timely approvals for material public-facing content, filming, claims, offers and operational changes when approval is required.

Lead handling

BPM/Omid remains responsible for sales conversations, consultations, management proposals, final pricing, management agreements and operational onboarding unless another workflow is separately agreed.

Filming availability

Video output depends on Omid/team availability, usable footage and timely participation in agreed filming or content-capture sessions.

9. Terms & Conditions

1. Term

The engagement is a 12-month partnership beginning on the date Phase 1 is accepted and the initial payment is successfully processed.

2. Billing Authorization

By accepting this agreement and completing Stripe Checkout, BPM authorizes the billing schedule stated in Section 8 using the payment method supplied to Stripe, subject to Stripe authentication requirements.

3. Scope & Prioritization

E4LA may adjust the mix, sequencing or cadence of individual deliverables when needed to prioritize quality, performance, business impact, platform constraints or changing evidence, while preserving the overall scope and objective of the engagement.

4. Deliverable Cadence

References such as approximately 12 social assets per month and typically 6–8 short-form videos are working targets rather than rigid minimum guarantees. Output may vary based on footage, approvals, platform performance, campaign needs and strategic priorities.

5. Third-Party Platforms

E4LA is not responsible for outages, algorithm changes, platform policy changes, account suspensions, rejected content, ranking volatility or limitations imposed by Google, Meta, email providers, AI/search platforms, Stripe or other third parties.

6. Performance

E4LA does not guarantee a specific ranking, lead count, client count, managed-property count or revenue result. E4LA's obligation is to perform the agreed services, monitor results and make reasonable evidence-based improvements.

7. No Legal Representation

E4LA provides growth, digital, marketing and automation services only. Content relating to tenant issues, notices, disputes or court-related property-management processes will be framed as BPM's operational experience and will not present E4LA or Omid as providing legal advice unless independently authorized to do so.

8. Performance Commission

No performance commission is due until a separate written addendum is accepted by both parties.

9. Intellectual Property

Upon payment of applicable fees, BPM may use final client-specific deliverables created for BPM. E4LA retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, frameworks, reusable systems, methodologies, prompts, automation architecture and general know-how.

10. Confidentiality

Both parties will use reasonable care to protect non-public business information received during the engagement and use it only for legitimate partnership work.

11. Data & Security

E4LA will use reasonable access controls and least-privilege practices. BPM should not provide unnecessary sensitive tenant, legal or financial data. High-impact automated actions require appropriate authorization.

12. Change Requests

Material work outside this scope—including extensive custom software, a full unrelated platform rebuild, substantial paid-media expansion, unrelated business projects or major new integrations—requires separate written approval and may require additional fees.

13. Delays

Delays caused by missing access, missing client information, delayed approvals, unavailable filming participants or third-party platform issues may shift timelines without changing payment obligations for work already scheduled or performed.

14. Failed Payments

If a scheduled payment fails or requires additional authentication, E4LA may pause work until the payment issue is resolved.

15. Cancellation / Early Termination

Any cancellation, refund or early-termination arrangement must be documented in writing by both parties. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, completed work and amounts already earned for performed services are not reversed solely because the engagement ends early.

16. Entire Scope

This agreement and any written addenda accepted by both parties define the operative scope for this engagement. Material changes should be documented in writing.
