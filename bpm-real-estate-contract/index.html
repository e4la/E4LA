Commercial Structure

The first six weeks are the launch period. After launch, the partnership moves into ongoing growth operations through the remainder of the 12-month term.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3$2,400

Strategy, audits, owner/investor journey, competitive research, lead-generation architecture, SEO/GEO opportunity research, CRM/attribution design, social strategy and Growth Blueprint.

Phase 2 · Weeks 4–6$2,400

Implementation and launch: priority website work, Rental Analysis funnel, tracking, Google/SEO/GEO foundation, CRM, email foundation, social launch system, video plan, AI Agent V1 and QA.

Phase 3 · After Week 6$2,900/month

Ongoing owner acquisition, website optimization, SEO/GEO, Google, Instagram, Facebook, short-form video, email, reviews, CRM, AI monitoring, competitor intelligence and quarterly reviews.

Core Scope

E4LA will operate an integrated owner-acquisition system focused on qualified property owners and new managed properties.

Lead Generation

High-intent Google search, Rental Analysis funnel, switch-manager campaigns, investor/landlord communities, out-of-area owners, professional referral partners, Instagram/Facebook acquisition and existing-lead reactivation where usable records exist.

Website & Conversion

Owner-first UX, investor journey, mobile optimization, conversion paths, Rental Analysis, CTA optimization and ongoing conversion improvements.

Search & Visibility

SEO, Local Search, Google Business Profile and GEO/AI-search optimization focused on owner intent and qualified demand.

Instagram, Facebook & Short-Form Video

Owner/investor content strategy, approximately 12 primary social assets per month as a working cadence, and typically 6–8 short-form video topics/productions per month depending on footage, availability and priorities.

CRM, Email & Follow-Up

Lead source tracking, qualification, pipeline stages, next actions, nurture/follow-up sequences and monitoring of stalled opportunities.

AI Growth & Revenue Agent

Website health, search opportunity, lead follow-up, competitor intelligence and growth summary monitoring.

Measurement

Primary reporting order: New Managed Properties → Signed Management Agreements → Qualified Owner Leads → Consultations. Marketing metrics are secondary diagnostics.

Terms & Conditions

1. Term

The engagement is a 12-month partnership beginning on the date Phase 1 is accepted and the initial payment is successfully processed.

2. Billing Schedule

$2,400 is due at acceptance. A second $2,400 payment is scheduled 21 days after the project start date. Beginning 42 days after the project start date, billing changes to $2,900 per month through the remainder of the 12-month term.

3. Scope & Prioritization

E4LA will perform the services described in this agreement and may adjust the mix or cadence of individual deliverables when needed to prioritize quality, performance and business impact while preserving the overall scope and objective.

4. Client Access & Approvals

BPM will provide reasonably timely access to required accounts, business information, approvals, brand assets and subject-matter input. Delays may shift implementation timing.

5. Third-Party Costs

Paid media, premium software, data providers, substantial API usage, SMS/calling services and other material third-party expenses are separate unless specifically stated and require approval before purchase.

6. Performance

E4LA does not guarantee a specific ranking, lead count, client count or revenue result. E4LA is responsible for building, operating, measuring and improving the agreed growth system using reasonable professional judgment and available data.

7. Performance Commission

Any performance commission for attributable new managed properties will be governed by a separate written addendum defining attribution, exclusions, multi-property owners, commission basis and payment timing. No performance commission is charged until that addendum is agreed in writing.

8. Intellectual Property

Upon payment of applicable fees, BPM may use final client-specific deliverables created for BPM. E4LA retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, frameworks, methodologies, reusable systems and general know-how.

9. Confidentiality

Both parties will use reasonable care to protect non-public business information received during the engagement and use it only for legitimate partnership work.

10. Change Requests

Material work outside the agreed scope, including extensive custom software, major unrelated platform builds or substantial paid-media expansion, requires separate written approval and may require additional fees.

11. Payment Authorization

By accepting this agreement and completing Stripe Checkout, BPM authorizes the billing schedule described above using the payment method supplied to Stripe, subject to Stripe authentication requirements.

12. Failed Payments / Early Termination

If a scheduled payment fails or requires additional authentication, work may be paused until resolved. Any refund, cancellation or early-termination arrangement must be documented in writing by both parties.

Acceptance & Payment
