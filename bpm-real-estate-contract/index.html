5. Measurement & Reviews

Monthly reviews start with business results rather than vanity metrics. Quarterly full evaluations occur at Months 3, 6, 9 and 12.

Monthly Growth Review

New managed properties, signed agreements, qualified owner leads, consultations, attribution, pipeline losses and estimated recurring management revenue added.

Quarterly Full Evaluation

Qualified leads, consultations, agreements, new managed properties, source/attribution, funnel losses, strongest channels, what to scale, what to change and what to stop.

The notice must clearly state the requested termination date and allow the parties a reasonable opportunity to address any stated service concern during the notice period. E4LA will continue performing the agreed services through the effective termination date unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

  • BPM remains responsible for fees earned and scheduled through the effective termination date, plus approved third-party costs or non-cancellable commitments incurred for BPM.
  • Amounts already paid for completed work, reserved capacity, or services already performed are non-refundable except where required by law or expressly agreed in writing.
  • No new recurring service fee will be charged for a service period beginning after the effective termination date.
  • After all undisputed amounts due are paid, E4LA will provide BPM's final paid client-specific deliverables and a reasonable handoff of agreed account/access information. E4LA retains its pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, systems and know-how as stated in Section 9.
