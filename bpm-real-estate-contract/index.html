1. Three Primary Business Goals

The work will be prioritized around three measurable growth paths rather than a broad list of disconnected marketing activities.

Goal 1Switch to BPM

Reach owners who are dissatisfied with their current property manager and convert qualified prospects into BPM management agreements.

Goal 2New Rental Owners

Reach rental-property owners actively looking for professional management and convert qualified inquiries into signed management agreements.

Goal 3Investment Opportunities

Position BPM and Omid in front of Realtors, investors and buyers through opportunity pages, search visibility, expert content and Instagram distribution.

2. Execution Roadmap

The website is redesigned without unnecessarily discarding existing SEO equity. Growth work then builds on the redesigned site and the three priority business goals.

Weeks 1–3Protect, Understand & Redesign
  • Audit current website UX, information architecture and conversion paths.
  • Document existing indexed URLs, search performance, metadata, internal links and other SEO assets before redesign.
  • Preserve high-value URLs where practical and plan 301 redirects only where URLs must change.
  • Define the three primary user journeys: Switch to BPM, New Rental Owner, and Investment Opportunity.
  • Redesign the website experience, navigation, messaging, trust elements and calls to action.
  • Prepare the Search / GEO / AI visibility structure for the new growth pages.
Weeks 3–6Build, Launch & Connect the Growth Paths
  • Implement the approved website redesign while protecting existing SEO value.
  • Create and optimize the three priority growth pathways and their supporting pages.
  • Build the Switch to BPM conversion path.
  • Build the New Rental Owner / Rental Analysis conversion path.
  • Build the Investment Opportunities hub and repeatable individual opportunity-page structure.
  • Set up conversion tracking and lead attribution for website, search and campaign sources.
  • Launch the redesigned experience and verify mobile, technical, indexing and conversion behavior.
From Week 6Ongoing Search, AI Visibility & Growth Operations
  • Improve visibility for the three business goals across Google and relevant AI/search experiences.
  • Create and optimize owner-focused search content, FAQs and landing pages.
  • Create Omid-led articles and market commentary that support investment opportunities and authority.
  • Publish and optimize individual investment-opportunity pages as opportunities become available.
  • Continuously improve website conversion, internal linking, search performance and attribution.
  • Use Instagram strategically to distribute properties and investment opportunities to relevant Realtors, investors and referral partners.

3. The Growth System

E4LA will operate one connected growth system rather than treating website, SEO, AI visibility, content and social media as separate projects.

Search / AI Visibility→Priority Growth Pages→Content & Opportunities→Instagram Distribution→Lead Capture→Attribution→Consultation / Deal
Website + UXProtect SEO while improving conversion

The redesign will prioritize user journeys, trust, navigation, mobile usability and conversion without unnecessarily replacing URLs or search equity that already work.

SEO + GEO / AI VisibilityBuild authority around the three business goals

Search and AI-visibility work will focus on useful, expert, locally relevant content and pages that directly support owner acquisition and investment opportunities.

Investment ContentTurn Omid's expertise into searchable authority

E4LA will develop opportunity pages, market commentary and articles that explain why a property, area or investment thesis may matter to investors and Realtors.

InstagramDistribution to Realtors and investors

Instagram work will focus primarily on properties, investment opportunities, market authority and Realtor/investor conversations. Follower count is not the primary KPI.

5. Performance Compensation

New Rental Owner$200 flat

Due for each E4LA-attributable new rental owner who signs a new BPM property-management agreement.

Switch to BPM$300 flat

Due for each E4LA-attributable owner who switches from another property manager to BPM and signs a BPM management agreement.

Investment Opportunity20% of actual commission received

Due on an attributable closed transaction from an investment opportunity where Omid/BPM receives a real-estate commission.

Attribution: A lead or transaction is attributable to E4LA when the relationship can reasonably be traced to E4LA-created or E4LA-managed website pages, search/AI visibility, content, Instagram campaigns, tracked forms, direct campaign outreach or an identifiable E4LA introduction. The $200 New Rental Owner fee and $300 Switch to BPM fee do not stack on the same owner/property; the applicable category is used.

7. Terms & Conditions

1. Term

The engagement is structured as a 12-month growth partnership. The initial 90 days are treated as the launch and stabilization period. After the initial 90 days, either party may terminate ongoing services with at least 30 days' written notice.

2. Scope & Prioritization

E4LA may adjust sequencing and tactical mix to prioritize the three agreed business goals, preserve working systems and respond to evidence, platform constraints and available client inputs without materially reducing the agreed scope.

3. Existing SEO & Website Risk

E4LA will use reasonable professional practices to preserve existing search value during redesign, including URL preservation or redirects where appropriate. Search rankings and traffic can fluctuate during website changes and cannot be guaranteed.

4. Performance

E4LA does not guarantee rankings, AI citations, lead volume, signed clients, property count, transaction closings or revenue. E4LA will perform the agreed work, monitor available evidence and make reasonable improvements.

5. Third-Party Platforms

E4LA is not responsible for outages, algorithm changes, ranking volatility, account restrictions or limitations imposed by Google, Meta, AI/search platforms, hosting providers, CMS platforms or other third parties.

6. Intellectual Property

Upon payment of applicable fees, BPM may use final client-specific deliverables. E4LA retains its pre-existing tools, templates, frameworks, reusable systems, methodologies, prompts, automation architecture and general know-how.

7. Confidentiality & Data

Both parties will use reasonable care to protect non-public business information. BPM should not provide unnecessary sensitive tenant, legal or financial information. E4LA will use reasonable access-control and least-privilege practices for systems it accesses.

8. Change Requests

Material work outside this scope requires separate written approval and may require additional fees.

9. Client-Caused Delays

Missing access, information, approvals, property materials, filming participation or third-party issues may shift timelines without changing fees for work already scheduled or performed.

10. Performance Fees

The performance-fee terms in Section 5 apply only when the applicable lead or transaction is reasonably attributable to E4LA under the attribution definition in this agreement.

11. Entire Agreement

This agreement and any later written addenda accepted by both parties define the operative scope and compensation. Material changes must be documented in writing.
